# Design: PULSE Forum 2026 story draw [21.10.2026]

## Intent

Before the first networking break on 28.10.2026 the host invites guests to take part digitally: scan a QR
code, share the most interesting or unexpected destination a new contact took them to, and enter a draw. One
winner is driven to the forum by SIXT the next morning, within Riga.

## Flow

0. Start, bilingual: PULSE mark, date and venue, the question in Latvian and English, LATVISKI / ENGLISH.
1. Category, one of eight: new contact, business growth, collaboration, an event, a new business idea, a career
   move, a trip, something else.
2. Story, 20-1500 characters.
3. Contact: first and last name, e-mail, phone [the pickup call], company [optional]; consent to the rules
   [required], consent to share the story with the first name only [optional]; a hidden honeypot field.
4. Thanks, shown only after the collector stored the entry. Variants: already in the draw [same e-mail],
   entries closed.

The answer queues on the phone and resends with the same code until the collector answers, so a dropped venue
connection neither loses an entry nor doubles it.

## Separation

The collector shares nothing with any other service: its own unit with a dynamic user, its own state
directory, its own SQLite file, its own port [8820], its own key file and its own public path
[`/pulse/`]. The site calls one address only [`frontend/config.js`].

## Look

From the PULSE brand book and pulseforum.co: coal stage, lime headlines and buttons, white text, orange labels;
the vertical line of the logo's L as the rail on every step; square bullets and markers, left aligned, square
brackets for short notes and no parentheses in copy; uppercase only for labels, dates and the headline face.
Body type Barlow Condensed [the brand's]; headlines Saira Extra Condensed, the closest open face to the brand's
Komu New C, to be swapped for Komu if licensed webfont files arrive. The PULSE mark is the brand book's own
vector; the SIXT logo is used as supplied, never recoloured.

## Organisers

A private link opens `admin.html` with a long random key in the fragment, so no login and no third-party
service is involved. The page lists every entry, counts per category, downloads Excel, opens and closes
entries, excludes a row from the draw, draws a winner [uniform, operating-system randomness, every draw logged
with its pool size] and, after the forum, deletes everything.

## Data

Name, e-mail, phone, company and story, used only for the draw and to call the winner; stored on a server in
the European Union; to be deleted by 30.11.2026.
